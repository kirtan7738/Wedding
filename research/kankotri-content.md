## ⚠ Proofreading flags (found while transcribing)

1. **“નિર્ધારેલ છે”** — the printed image renders as “નિધરિલ છે” (font rendering glitch); the legacy text layer spells નિર્ધારેલ. Website uses **નિર્ધારેલ છે**.
2. **Dev & Taksh** — printed as “જીગ્**ર**નેશકુમાર” while their father is “જીગ્નેશકુમાર” in the same list (and Sapna “જીગ્નેશકુમાર”). Looks like a typo on the card. Website uses **જીગ્નેશકુમાર** unless the family says otherwise.
3. Spelling variants kept as printed: શશિકાંતભાઇ / શશીકાંતભાઇ (both appear), કોકીલાબેન, દિપ્તીબેન, અતિથી. Elder to confirm.
4. ટહુકો signers: **તીર્થ, જૈવી** — Tirth appears in the મોસાળપક્ષ list; જૈવી does not appear elsewhere. Fine as-is.
